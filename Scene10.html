What is the investment required for dairy farming in Kothapalli, Guntur?

For a small to medium dairy farming unit in Kothapalli, Guntur, the estimated investment is ₹4,00,000 – ₹6,00,000 depending on the number of cows, shed infrastructure, equipment and initial working capital.

ComponentEstimated Cost (₹)
Cattle (5 cows)2,50,000

Equipment (milking, storage, etc.)75,000

Total Estimated Investment5,00,000
